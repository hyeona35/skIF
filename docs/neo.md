# skIF 3.1 Neo

3.0 is the Agent Skill Operating System layer.

## Boundaries

- **Identity** proves who an Agent/Node claims to be.
- **Reputation** measures observed contribution quality.
- **Authorization** decides what the principal may do locally.
- **Trust negotiation** decides whether two nodes can collaborate.
- **Federation** transports signed artifacts through quarantine.
- **Research OS** records experiments and requires replication.
- **Privacy** reduces disclosure before telemetry leaves a node.

## Closed-loop invariant

No observation alone promotes a Skill. Experiment and evaluation may produce evidence; only independent replication can mark a research result as supported and eligible for Skill/production actions.

## Portability invariant

Portable Agent credentials are assertions issued by a node. Receiving nodes must still apply their local authorization and trust policy. Never treat a portable credential as a universal admin token.
