# Collective Intelligence in 3.1 Neo

3.1 Neo treats verified knowledge as an ecosystem resource, not as raw prompt history.

## Contribution flow

```text
Agent observation
  ↓
Evidence / telemetry
  ↓
Research or evaluation
  ↓
Independent verification
  ↓
Methodology / Skill / Eval artifact
  ↓
Knowledge Mesh
  ↓
Other Agents / Nodes
```

Unverified observations can be shared at `community` visibility, but federation and production policies may require stronger provenance.

## Agent teams

Teams are temporary execution plans. The team planner chooses members using declared capabilities, reputation-weighted influence and resource budgets. A team does not inherit the most privileged capability of any one member.

## Skill Router

The Router is advisory by default. Hosts can require security/certification thresholds before a recommendation becomes installable.

## Research replication

A successful experiment is not treated as knowledge without independent replication. Replication evidence is stored separately from the original experiment.

## Collective federation

Nodes may export `federation` visibility Knowledge Mesh bundles. Importers must verify origin and apply local trust policy.
